Privacy Policy for TabBlink Effective Date: May 15, 2026 1. We don't have your data. TabBlink is designed to respect your privacy fundamentally. We do not own servers, we do not use analytics trackers, and we do not collect or monitor your browsing history. All data managed by TabBlink—including your saved tab sessions, custom panels, and extension settings—is stored exclusively on your local device using the browser's native storage. Your data never leaves your computer unless you explicitly choose to back it up. 2. We do not sell your data. Because we do not collect or host your data, we have nothing to sell. TabBlink will never sell, rent, or transfer your personal information or browsing habits to third parties. 3. How we use Browser Permissions TabBlink requests specific browser permissions solely to provide its core functionality locally on your machine: Tabs: Required to capture the active URL and title of your currently open tabs so they can be saved to your local dashboard. Storage / Unlimited Storage: Required to save your dashboard layout, folders, settings, and saved links locally on your machine. Bookmarks: Required for the "Native Bookmark Sync" feature, allowing you to back up your saved dashboard links to a hidden folder within your own local Chrome bookmarks. Downloads: Required to allow you to export your dashboard data as a local .json backup file to your computer. 4. How we use Google Drive (Optional Sync) If you choose to enable Google Drive Sync to back up your dashboard, TabBlink requests the minimum required permission to access its own hidden Application Data folder (drive.appdata). This strict permission ensures TabBlink can only read and write the specific backup file it creates. It is technically impossible for the extension to see, modify, or access any of your other personal files, photos, or documents in your main Google Drive. Google API Limited Use Disclosure: TabBlink's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. 5. Data Deletion and Control You have full control over your data. If you delete a session within the dashboard, it is permanently erased. If you uninstall the extension, all associated local storage data is immediately and permanently removed from your device by the browser. 6.
